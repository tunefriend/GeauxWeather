Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026 · App: GeauxWeather (com.geauxweather.app)

GeauxWeather is free software. It does not show ads, does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not require an account.

Data we collect

The developers of GeauxWeather do not operate a backend that collects personal data from the app. Weather and map features contact public third-party services you choose to use by using the app.

Network services used by the app

  • Open-Meteo — forecasts and weather data
  • RainViewer — precipitation radar tiles
  • NHC — tropical cyclone tracks / cones
  • NWS — severe weather polygons and alerts
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — place search and reverse geocoding
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles — base map

Those providers process the requests your device sends (for example location coordinates for a forecast). Their own privacy policies apply to those services.

Location

Location permission is optional. You can search for a city by name without granting GPS. When you use “current location”, coordinates stay on device for fetching weather and are sent only to the services above as needed for that feature.

Local storage

Settings, saved places, and last-known weather may be stored on your device (app preferences / local storage). This data is not uploaded to a GeauxWeather server.

Notifications

Optional severe-storm alerts use Android notifications. You can deny notification permission; the rest of the app continues to work.

Website

This website (geauxweather.com) is a static landing page. It does not set advertising cookies or use third-party analytics.
