PureSky is a free weather app with no ads and no tracking.

Features:
• Current conditions, hourly and 10-day forecast (Open-Meteo)
• Rain radar map with timeline (RainViewer)
• Drop a pin on the map — nearest city name, save to Places
• Places list with temperature and sky condition
• Long-press a place to set your default city
• Refresh uses GPS when available, otherwise your default

Data from Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap. No account required.
