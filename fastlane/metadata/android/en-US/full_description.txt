GeauxWeather is a free weather app with no ads and no tracking.

Features:
• Current conditions, hourly and 10-day forecast (Open-Meteo)
• Maps: rain radar (RainViewer), wind field, fog, hurricane cone/track (NHC), tornado & severe alerts (NWS)
• Drop a pin on the map — nearest city name, save to Places
• Places list; long-press to set your default city
• Optional severe storm alerts for your home or default location
• Weather sky backgrounds (day/night), home widget, status-bar temperature
• Feedback and Liberapay donate

Data from Open-Meteo, RainViewer, NHC, NWS, and OpenStreetMap. No account required.
